# ONE PLACE — DOCUMENT 07

# UI/UX DESIGN SYSTEM & COMPLETE PAGE-BY-PAGE SPECIFICATION

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Development Ready
**Product:** One Place
**Scope:** MVP + Post-MVP
**Primary platform:** Responsive Web Application
**Design priority:** Simple, human, trustworthy, calm, fast

---

# 1. Design Philosophy

One Place should **not look like another social network, marketplace overloaded with cards, or AI product**.

The visual feeling should be:

> **"I came here because I need something. Help me find it."**

The interface should feel:

* calm
* mature
* approachable
* intelligent
* trustworthy
* local
* human
* uncluttered

The product should not scream:

> AI! AI! AI!

AI is infrastructure.

The customer experience is:

> **Find → Understand → Talk → Connect**

---

# 2. Core Design Principle

The most important design rule:

> **Every screen should answer one question.**

Examples:

Homepage:

> What are you looking for?

Search:

> Which service can help you?

Business profile:

> Can this business help me?

Chat:

> Can I get an answer?

Voice:

> Can I talk to someone?

Business dashboard:

> What needs my attention?

* fewer repetitive calls

## Business staff

Employees who handle:

* chats
* requests
* customer questions
* service information

Responsible for:

* verification
* moderation

# 4. Visual Personality

One Place should feel closer to:

**Airbnb + Stripe + modern local directory**

than:

**Facebook + Yelp + chatbot dashboard.**

The UI should have plenty of breathing room.
